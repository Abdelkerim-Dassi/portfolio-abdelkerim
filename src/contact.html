How it runs

Thirty minutes. You describe the problem, I tell you honestly whether it is one I should be taking.

A written scope with a first deliverable small enough to prove the approach. If it does not work, you find that out early and cheaply.

Ship it
Into production, in front of real users. A demo that works 95% of the time is not the deliverable.

Hand over
Documentation and a working session with your team. The point is that the system keeps running after I stop invoicing.
