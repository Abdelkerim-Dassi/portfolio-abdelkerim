Personal projects

01

Signal Desk: Crypto Decision Desk

Designed, built and deployed alone. A rule engine scores 18 coins from 0 to 100 behind a market-regime gate; an LLM turns those numbers into a briefing and answers questions in a streaming chat grounded in them. The LLM is never called on the 2-minute refresh, so idle traffic costs nothing. Backtested over 35K+ coin-days since 2021: setups rated strong averaged +8.6% over 30 days, against +3.6% for all signals. FastAPI · React · Redis · Vercel.
